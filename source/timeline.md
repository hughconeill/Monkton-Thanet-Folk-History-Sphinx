# Timeline

A quick-reference list of key dates. Full detail and sources for each
entry are in the relevant [History](history/index.md) chapter.

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:widths: 15 85

* - Date
  - Event
* - c. 1250s
  - First documented reference to Monkton, in a manorial roll.
* - c. 1300s
  - Parish church believed built (dating uncertain — see
    [Origins](history/origins.md)).
* - 1600s
  - Earliest surviving estate map of the village.
* - 1750s–1960s
  - Watermill in continuous operation — the village's main employer
    for much of this period.
* - 1914–1918
  - First World War; [N] men from the parish served.
* - 1939–1945
  - Second World War; [N] men and women from the parish served.
* - 1960s onward
  - Post-war housing development; village shifts from farming
    community to commuter village.
```
