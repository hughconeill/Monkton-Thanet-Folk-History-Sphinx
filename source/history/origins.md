# Origins and Early Settlement

The earliest documented reference to Monkton appears in a 13th-century
manorial roll, though the name suggests the settlement grew up around a
watermill on the brook that still runs through the village
today.[^manor-roll]

## The manor

Land in and around the village belonged to the [Manor Name] estate
from at least the medieval period. Court rolls from this period
record tenant names, rents, and disputes, and are one of the richest
sources for the village's early population.[^court-rolls]

```{admonition} Conflicting evidence
:class: warning

Two 14th-century sources give different founding dates for the parish
church — this is a common issue with medieval records. Where sources
disagree, note both here rather than picking one silently.
```
