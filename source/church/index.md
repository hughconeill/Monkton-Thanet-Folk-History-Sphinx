# The Church

St Mary Magdalene, Monkton — the smallest of Thanet's seven ancient
churches, and one of the oldest. This section brings together the
church's architectural history, its bells, its clergy back to 1291,
its war memorial, and detailed records of memorials both inside the
building and across its churchyard.
